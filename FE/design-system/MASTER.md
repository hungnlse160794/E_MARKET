# Design System: FreshCart Pro (V8 - Chromatic Botanical Luxe)

## Brand Identity
**Concept**: "The Spectrum Ecosystem"
**Style**: Multi-chromatic accents on a Luminous Pearl base. Primary brand color is **Light Emerald**, but supported by a rich secondary palette to prevent monotony. 
**Keywords**: Vibrant, Diverse, Fresh, Precision, Multi-node, Luminous.

## Core Visual Palette
- **Primary (Brand)**: `#10B981` (Emerald - Vitality & Freshness)
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with subtle `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) sections.
- **Spectrum Accents**:
  - **Violet-Tech**: `#8B5CF6` (For synthetic/high-tech nodes)
  - **Amber-Organic**: `#F59E0B` (For natural/organic products)
  - **Azure-Sync**: `#3B82F6` (For logistics/global connectivity)
  - **Rose-Vital**: `#EC4899` (For premium/specialty items)
- **Typography**: `#0F172A` (Slate-900 - Deep, clear contrast).

## Design Tokens (Tailwind)
- **Primary-Accent**: `bg-emerald-500` (Main brand marker)
- **Category-Violet**: `bg-violet-50 text-violet-600 border-violet-100`
- **Category-Amber**: `bg-amber-50 text-amber-600 border-amber-100`
- **Category-Emerald**: `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-600 border-emerald-100`
- **Glass**: `bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-2xl border border-slate-100 shadow-[0_30px_60px_rgba(15,23,42,0.05)]`
- **Radius**: `3xl` (24px) - Soft organic professional.

## Typography
- **Heading**: 'Outfit', sans-serif - Bold weights, balanced tracking.
- **Body**: 'Inter', sans-serif - High legibility, medium weight for descriptions.

## Motion Principles
- **Prism**: Subtle color-shifting glows on interaction.
- **Elevate**: Smooth lifts with multi-colored shadow tints.
- **Sync**: Coordinated transitions between color nodes.

## Role Specific UI
- **User (Customer)**: Multi-colored categories, vibrant and engaging.
- **Shop Owner**: Color-coded analytics (Green for growth, Blue for orders, Violet for stock).
- **Admin**: Master hub with full chromatic range representing different system sectors.
